# Bracket 3 — NZ land and freshwater bodies

**~5 contacts, tracker Tiers 2–4.** NZ Landcare Trust, QEII National Trust,
Manaaki Whenua – Landcare Research, Our Land and Water National Science Challenge,
WSP New Zealand.

## Who
The New Zealand institutions between landowners, science and freshwater policy —
catchment group networks, covenant bodies, Crown research, and the national
science programme.

## What they measure
Whether a tool will actually be used by the people they support, most of whom are
farmers or covenant holders rather than GIS users. Whether the method stands up
to a Crown research reviewer. Whether it duplicates something already funded.

## What they distrust
Another tool. They have seen many, most abandoned within two years by whoever
built them for a thesis or a grant. Being pitched rather than consulted.

## Facts that prove it, in order
1. `TF-11` **LINZ LiDAR — this is the one bracket where it leads.** For most of NZ
   the data is already there and free, which is the difference between a tool
   their people can use and one they cannot.
2. `TF-30`–`TF-33` CAMELS-NZ. **A New Zealand validation dataset for a New Zealand
   audience** — this bracket will recognise it, and Manaaki Whenua and Our Land
   and Water in particular will know exactly what it is worth. Always with `TF-33`.
3. `TF-26` ponding volume in litres — the number that lands on a farm bonnet.
4. `TF-02` free and open source: nothing to procure, nothing to renew.
5. `TF-72` and `TF-73` the honest install and documentation gaps, which matter
   here because they determine whether it can be recommended to a farmer today.

## Opener mechanism — the conversation on the farm
Their work is translation. Open inside it.

> The hard part is never explaining where the water goes. It is explaining why
> the bit up the hill is the reason.

Or, for Manaaki Whenua and Our Land and Water, the method opener from bracket 5 is
the better fit — read the tracker's *Why It's a Fit* and choose.

## Limits to state
`TF-70` in plain words, plus `TF-72`: installation is still manual, which is a
real barrier for the people they would hand it to. Naming that before they find
it is worth more than hiding it.

## The ask
Half an hour, and whether it would survive being shown to a farmer or a covenant
holder — a harder test than any technical one.

## Never say
- Any nutrient, nitrogen, sediment-loss or water-quality claim. It models surface
  water movement and nothing else.
- Anything about compliance, consent conditions, or freshwater regulation.
- "Stakeholder", "solution", "engagement".
- That it is validated. One NZ catchment is. `TF-71`.
